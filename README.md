## 🌐 Overview

**PolarCross** is an integrated digital platform designed to make India's polar science, expeditions and research more accessible, discoverable and engaging.

The platform brings together polar expedition reports, scientific publications, datasets, photographs, videos and institutional activities into a unified knowledge repository.

PolarCross also provides an AI-powered outreach layer that transforms complex scientific material into accessible, audience-specific content for websites and social media while maintaining a connection to the original research sources.

> **Explore. Discover. Understand. Share.**

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

### SIH26063

> **Integrated Polar Science Outreach, Knowledge Repository and Media Dissemination Portal**

India's polar research generates a large amount of valuable scientific knowledge through expeditions, publications, datasets, photographs, videos and institutional activities.

However, this information can be difficult for students, educators, researchers and the general public to discover and consume through a unified experience.

Furthermore, converting technical scientific material into accessible public-facing content requires significant manual effort.

PolarCross addresses this gap by combining **knowledge management, discovery, visualization and AI-assisted science communication** in one platform.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

PolarCross provides a centralized digital gateway for India's polar research ecosystem.

### Core capabilities

- 📚 **Polar Knowledge Repository**
  - Expedition reports
  - Scientific publications
  - Datasets
  - Photographs
  - Videos
  - Institutional activities

- 🔎 **Unified Search & Discovery**
  - Search across research resources
  - Filter by expedition, year, region, research area and content type
  - Discover related research and expedition material

- 🧭 **Expedition-Centric Exploration**
  - Explore individual expeditions
  - Connect research outputs, locations and multimedia
  - Present expedition information through a unified experience

- 🤖 **AI Outreach Studio**
  - Transform scientific material into accessible content
  - Generate website articles and social-media posts
  - Adapt content for different audiences and platforms
  - Maintain source references for generated content

- 🗺️ **Interactive Polar Map**
  - Explore Indian polar research locations
  - Visualize stations and expedition activity geographically

- 📰 **Latest Research & Updates**
  - Recent expeditions
  - New publications
  - Research highlights
  - Institutional updates
  - Multimedia content

- 📊 **Research Analytics**
  - Repository statistics
  - Expedition insights
  - Publication and media distribution
  - Research activity visualization

## 🧠 AI-Powered Outreach

A major component of PolarCross is its **AI Outreach Studio**.

Scientific information is often written for specialized audiences. PolarCross enables researchers and science communicators to select trusted research material and transform it into content suitable for different audiences.
